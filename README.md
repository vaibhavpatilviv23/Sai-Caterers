# 🍽️ Sai Caterers – Mumbai-Based Catering Service  

Sai Caterers is a **Mumbai-based catering service** specializing in **weddings and large events**, offering a wide variety of **traditional and contemporary food options**. This repository contains the **front-end website** developed to showcase their **menu, services, and brand identity**.  

Although the business is no longer actively updated due to lost contact, this repository remains an **archive** of the work done.

## 📌 Project Highlights
- **Showcased Catering Services** – Designed a professional website for customer engagement  
- **Menu Categories & Layout** – Presented dishes across different cuisines with structured pages  
- **Brand Identity** – Featured logo, images, and business details for a polished digital presence  

## 🚀 Technologies Used
- **HTML** – Structured web pages for clear navigation  
- **CSS** – Styled elements to enhance user experience  


## 🔗 Archive & Legacy
Sai Caterers provided **quality catering services for major events**, and this website was created to help showcase their offerings.
